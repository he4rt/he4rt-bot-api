# Config

## Fun facts are only for recurring contributors

People listed in `config/fun-facts.php` must already have a track record of
recurring contributions to this repository. Do not add someone based on a
single PR, a one-off commit, or just being a maintainer/admin without
sustained contribution history. Check their commit/PR history before adding
an entry.
